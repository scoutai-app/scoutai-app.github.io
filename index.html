How it works

Three clicks from question to answer

No copying and pasting. No prompts to learn.

1

Open Scout

Click the Scout icon in your toolbar. It opens in Chrome's side panel, next to the page you're on.

2

Capture

Click the camera to capture the tab. Add more tabs to compare them, or capture a full page.

3

Ask

Type a question, or tap a one-click action like Summarize or Compare. The answer appears in seconds.

What you can do

One assistant for everything you read online

Summarize

Get the point of any page in seconds

Long articles, reports and docs become a few clear takeaways, so you know what matters before you read on.

  • Research papers, news and long reports
  • Full-page capture for long pages (Pro)
Compare

Line up several pages side by side

Capture a few tabs and Scout turns them into one clear comparison table. Products, prices, plans, places.

  • Up to 15 screenshots in one question
  • Spot the differences without switching tabs
Extract

Turn charts and dashboards into numbers

Pull the data out of charts, tables and dashboards, even when you can't select the text. Ready to paste into a spreadsheet.

  • Charts, tables and dashboards on any site
  • Copy the answer or export it as a PDF
Explain

Contracts, terms, statements and technical pages, explained simply, with examples. Just ask the way you'd ask a colleague.

  • Follow-up questions in the same chat
  • Your conversations are saved in Scout
